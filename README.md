# **My Favorite Movie is *Iron Man 3***

## **About the Movie**
Iron Man 3 (2013) follows Tony Stark after the events of The Avengers, where he suffers from Insomnia and post-traumautic
stress disorder. A series of bombings attributed to a terrorist known as the Mandarin puts the United States on high alert. When Stark’s friend Happy Hogan is injured, Tony publicly challenges the Mandarin, prompting an attack that destroys his Malibu mansion and leaves him stranded with only a malfunctioning prototype suit.
